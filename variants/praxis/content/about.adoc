== About doit-ssg

doit-ssg is part of the poly-ssg ecosystem, demonstrating static site
generation in Io.

=== Why Io?

Io is a pure prototype-based object-oriented language inspired by
Smalltalk, Self, and Lua. Its minimalist syntax (no keywords, just
messages) and homoiconic nature make it highly expressive. Io’s
prototype system allows elegant modeling of content inheritance and
template composition without traditional class hierarchies.
