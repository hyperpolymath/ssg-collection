== About shift-ssg

shift-ssg is part of the poly-ssg ecosystem, demonstrating static site
generation in Wren.

=== Why Wren?

Wren is a small, fast, class-based scripting language designed for
embedding. Its clean syntax inspired by Lua and Smalltalk makes it
readable and expressive. Wren’s fiber-based concurrency model offers
interesting possibilities for parallel content processing during site
builds.
